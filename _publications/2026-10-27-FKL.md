excerpt: 'Kalah (a.k.a. Mancala) is a two-player game of perfect information that has been a popular game for over half a century despite a strong first player advantage. Previous AI research concerns Kalah with empty captures, yet there is evidence that original rules disallow empty captures. In this paper, we present the first analysis of Kalah without empty captures, initial game states that are fair, as well as optimal play insights from analysis of optimal and suboptimal states.'
